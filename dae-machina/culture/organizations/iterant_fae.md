# Iterant Fae

The vast majority of fae were born in one of the traditional fae communities
 - Daal'fae caravans
 - Midh'fae clans
 - etc

A moderate number do wind up elsewhere, but nature of 'fae genetics and wide variety of 'fae types means that multigenerational 'fae families in predominantly non-fae communites are exceptionally rare.
Thye main exceptions are the Xencha Defiant and the Sachean Diarchy, which maintain a relatively even popilation split of 'fae and non-'fae.

Ael'fae who live outside of their traditional communities generally gravitate towards diverse cities that are already friendly to outsiders, but you will occasionally run into a lone 'fae in some random backwater town.
Cities with notable 'fae populations include:

 - Most of the Free Cities, especially Elgate, Haven, Eaosport and Arcmuth (Mainly Daal'fae who have left a caravan, along with a moderate number of Xhal'fae displaced by the Riftscape. A small population of Merfae are present in Haven and Eaosport)
 - Many Riftlander cities (A fairly even mix of Caravan Daal'fae, Hyenid Daal'fae, and Feline Xhal'fae)
 - Many Drakkar clans, especially the more northen ones (Primarly wolf midh'fae, with a handful of bears and seals)
 - Druidic communities on the Draisblodh isles (Slightly more even mix of midh'fae than the Drakkar, but the majority are still wolves)
 - Dragonhalls (Mainly feline Xhal'fae and Xhao'fae. A small number of Thal'fae)
 - The Queen's Valley druids in southeastern Quon Xi (Thal'fae, Xhal'fae, and Xhao'fae)
 - Mountain monastaries between the southern valleys of Quon Xi (Mainly Thal'fae, with a small handful of Xhal'fae)

Most of the 'fae living in these cities have naturalized into the culture of their neighbors, though do tent to retain certain quirks and limitations.
Due to the shape of their mouths, Canid and Hyenid Daal'fae, and Feline Xhal'fae have difficulty speaking Eaosian, and as such typically communicate by sign language. Several of the common sign language dialects in the Free Cities originated from sailors and 
dockworkers, so these types of 'fae have found integrating into those lines of work especially easy, and are therefore most commonly found in the waterfront regions.

While most 'fae don't have any sort of nudity taboo, Daal'fae living in the cities have largely adapted to local norms, althoguh they still may wear unusual styles that are more comfortable for their anatomy.
City Xhal'fae mostly reject such norms - while they are much more comfortable with the trappings of Eaosian civilization, they still don't tend to wear much of anything. With the increased volume of Xhal'fae refugees
moving to cities after the cataclysm, this attitude is slowly shifting the norms of a number of coastal Elyos cities among both 'fae and non-fae.  

In such cities, minor 'fae physical characteristics will often show up without direct 'fae ancestry, having skipped a few generations.
