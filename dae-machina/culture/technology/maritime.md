# Maratime Technology

Sea travel - relatively sophisticated sailing ships - 100 - 200 km/day
  Sea - 9 - 18.5 km/h;  100 - 200 km/day; **150** average
  Upriver 5.6 km/h; **60** km/day for major rivers, 15 - 30 for smaller rivers.
  Downriver 11 - 22 km/h ; **120** - 240 km/day
  

Notable Maratime Powers

Dhagian Isles & Drakkar

South Dhagian - only of these with long-range sailing ships that regularly goes further south than Teythmos

Elyos Republic

Merchant fleets
Small navy, mostly defending from Drakkar and other piracy along the coast.

Free Cities

Haven is a notable shipbuilding power, and supplies ships to Free Cities and Elyos Republic - consequently these tend to have similar designs
Eaosport is slightly smaller but still notable. Maintains a navy to defend against piracy and other ilicit activities out of Blackport.
Blackport - mostly loosely affiliated pirate crews using stolen ships from Free cities. Black company maintains a small fleet based out of the city for transport and defense.
No real shipbuilding capabilities (no trees, little room), but does maintain shipyards for maintenance and repair.


Sachean Diarcy - Large fleet. Most long-range ships operate under a commision from the crown, so there's a great deal of uniformity among the ships seen by outsiders.
However, shorter range transport shows a great deal of variety. Open bottoms and catamarans are common. Larger vessels often contain submerged quarters for merfae.

Shorter range ships are often accompanied by merfae.

Handful of leviathans outfitted with merfae settlements.


Riftlanders.

Light, durable, coastal ships, typically single-deck and single-hulled, sometimes with an enclosed cabin. Mix of oars and sails. Mast can generally be lowered relatively easily (though the mechanism varies)
Some unusual designs, e.g.
 - Canvas hull with horizontal wings that can glide off of floating islands to safely land in the ocean below
 - Various magic-assisted designes capable of limited flight, sailing up waterfalls, etc.
 - Variety of folding sail designs - varios ribbed semi-circle sails.
 
No real navy, but most larger ships carry light armaments to fend of pirates and other dangers - Light balistas, mounted crossbows, magical weapon emplacemts scavenged from Uon ruins, that sort of thing.

Some groups of Xeres reborn have short-range rading ships and fishing boats, outside of this there's little in the way of maratime tech out of the shards of Xuon

Dragonhalls doesn't have a proper navy, mostly just fishing boats (and not many of those - more often trade with the coastal Xhal villages that aren't really part of the nation.
Still capable of projecting significant ocean power, as a half-dozen dragons can take on just about any fleet.
 
Large Xhal city west of dragonhalls has a notable navy capable of reaching across Kita Lau.
 Some nations on the other isles have similar capabilites, moreso in the eastern regions
 
Further west is controlled by Quon Xi, which has the single largest (and most advanced) navy in Dael. Multiple decks and three (or more) masts are common.
Mainly focused on the eastern half of the continent and the Lauan territories. Kaldean nations keep the western coastline well-defended, and further into the ___ Sea, the influence of Chasmlight makes long-range travel very dangerous

Kaldean nations have a small defensive navy - can't effectively challenge Quon Xi, but enough to provide a mobile defensive force to fend off marritiem assaults.
Focused on fast transport ships, and ship-sinker vessels with rams (e.g bireme-like design). Supplemented by a well maintained network of coastal towers that serve as both lighthouses and signal towers.



'jinn have little in the way of what one would consider a ship, but do have a substantial maratime presence, backed primarily by the ocean 'jinn.
For general travel, the 'jinn mainly use heavily enchanted floating pavilions, which can also serve as a pwerful maritime force.

Aquatic fishing constructs



Less ships, and more floating outposts
Most undersea settlements have some sort of floating surface area for non-aquatic visitors to land at.
Some of these are mobile outposts in their own right, and be used as ocean transport.

More traditional Barges / floating docks / etc do exist, mainly used to provide more waterfront room during coastal celebrations
And occasionally for transport

Still do have a handful of more traditional ships for exporation and transport around the eastern tip of Eaos-Mhidda
Tend to be archaic designs enhanced by magic.
