# Calender

The unique cosmology of this world leads to an equally unique calender.

Each day is 24 hours long.
At the equator, dawn is at 06:00 and dusk is at 18:00. This remains the case the further north or south you travel, but the sun does not reach as high in the sky. 
The poles are in a constant state of dusk, with the sun and other greater spheres always drifting near the horizon
Each week (i.e. "hekad") is 6 days long. This is the same as the orbital period of the
Sun and the Darksun.

Each month (i.e. "kahda") is 36 days (6 weeks). This is the orbital period of the

coincides with a full alignment of the three moons.

## Draconic Reckoning

Dragons, with their longer lifespan, also utilize intervals of 756 years, based on the cycle of their great gatherings, and counted from the Second Dragon accords, in 200,000,000 BC, 264,550 Gatherings ago.
They subdivide even larger intervals into Gathering's of Gatherings, (756 squared, or 571,536 convergences). The last Gathering of Gathering's was in 534,434 BC, and was the 349th one.
They don't track months or weeks, but will sometimes split years into two based on the mid-year convergence.

A full draconic date looks like "The 749th convergence of the 706th Gathering since the 349th Gathering of Gatherings", though typically the last clause is ommited.
Often, the number of the last gathering is replaced with it's location, so something like "The 749th convergence of the Dragonhalls Gathering" would be more typical.
